CraftLayer Studio designs and engineers websites, SaaS products and AI-powered applications for founders and growing businesses. It is led by founder and engineer [Shaswat Raj](https://github.com/playlikeboy).

## What we build

- **Websites:** marketing sites, landing pages and content sites that load fast and rank well.
- **SaaS products:** from first version to a product people pay for.
- **AI products:** LLM features, agents and AI-assisted workflows.
- **E-commerce:** storefronts, carts and checkout flows.
- **Dashboards and admin panels:** internal views over your data.
- **Booking systems:** schedules, reservations and appointment requests.
- **Custom web apps:** when an off-the-shelf tool does not fit.
- **API integrations:** connecting the services your business already uses.
- **MCP servers and automation:** tools that let AI agents and scripts act on your systems.
- **Internal tools:** the small apps that save a team hours every week.

## Who we work with

Founders, startups, small and medium businesses, and product teams that need a working product, not just a design file.

## Technologies we use in real work

Next.js · React · TypeScript · PostgreSQL · Redis · Cloudflare Workers · Hono · Model Context Protocol (MCP) · Python · GitHub Actions
